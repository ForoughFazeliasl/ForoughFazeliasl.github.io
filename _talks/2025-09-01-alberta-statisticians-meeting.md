**Invited Talk** at the **45th Annual Meeting of Alberta Statisticians**, held at the University of Alberta, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric framework for differentially private generative modeling. The proposed approach leverages Bayesian nonparametric learning to generate high-quality synthetic tabular data while providing rigorous differential privacy guarantees. The presentation covered the underlying methodology, theoretical privacy analysis, and experimental results demonstrating the utility of the proposed framework.
