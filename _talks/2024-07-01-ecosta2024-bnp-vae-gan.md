**Invited Talk** at the **7th International Conference on Econometrics and Statistics (EcoSta 2024)**, held at Beijing Normal University, Beijing, China.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric framework that integrates variational autoencoders and generative adversarial networks through the Wasserstein distance and Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD). The proposed approach combines Bayesian nonparametric learning with deep generative modeling to improve sample quality, training stability, and distribution matching for synthetic data generation. The presentation discussed the theoretical foundations of the framework together with experimental results demonstrating its effectiveness across a range of data generation tasks.
