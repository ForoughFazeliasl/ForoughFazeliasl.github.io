**Conference Talk** at the **EAC-ISBA 2023 Conference**, hosted by the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Qingdao University, China.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric perspective on training generative adversarial networks (GANs). The proposed framework integrates Bayesian nonparametric learning into adversarial training to improve robustness, uncertainty quantification, and distribution matching. The presentation discussed the theoretical foundations of the approach together with empirical results demonstrating its effectiveness for deep generative modeling.
