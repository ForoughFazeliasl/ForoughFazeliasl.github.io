**Conference Talk** at **BAYSM 2023 (Bayesian Young Statisticians Meeting)**, the official conference of the junior section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis (j-ISBA), hosted by the University of Connecticut.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric goodness-of-fit testing framework based on the Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD). The proposed methodology combines Bayesian nonparametric inference with kernel-based discrepancy measures to provide a flexible and robust approach for assessing distributional goodness-of-fit. The presentation discussed the theoretical properties of the proposed test together with numerical studies illustrating its performance.
