**Workshop Talk** at the **NeurIPS 2024 Workshop on Bayesian Decision-making and Uncertainty (BDU)**, held in conjunction with the **38th Annual Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2024)** in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric framework for synthetic data generation based on the Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD). The proposed methodology combines Bayesian nonparametric learning with kernel-based distribution matching to generate high-quality synthetic data while preserving the underlying data distribution. The presentation discussed the theoretical foundations of the approach together with empirical results demonstrating its effectiveness for synthetic data generation.
