**Invited Talk** at the **44th Annual Meeting of Alberta Statisticians**, held at the University of Calgary, Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric framework for synthetic data generation based on push-forward measures. The proposed methodology leverages Bayesian nonparametric learning to construct flexible generative models that preserve complex distributional characteristics while providing principled uncertainty quantification. The presentation highlighted the theoretical foundations of the approach together with experimental results demonstrating its effectiveness for synthetic data generation.
