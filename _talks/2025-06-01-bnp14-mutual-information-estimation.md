**Invited Talk** at the **14th International Conference on Bayesian Nonparametrics (BNP14)**, held at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), USA.

This talk presented a deep Bayesian nonparametric framework for robust mutual information estimation. The proposed approach combines neural mutual information estimation with Bayesian nonparametric learning to improve robustness, quantify uncertainty, and reduce sensitivity to sampling variability.
