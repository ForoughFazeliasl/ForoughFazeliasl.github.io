**Invited Session Talk** at the **2026 Statistical Society of Canada (SSC) Annual Meeting**, held at McMaster University, Hamilton, Ontario, Canada.

This talk presented a Bayesian nonparametric framework for differentially private tabular data generation. The proposed methodology combines Bayesian nonparametric learning with generative modeling to provide strong privacy guarantees while preserving statistical utility. The presentation covered the theoretical foundations of the proposed mechanism together with experimental results demonstrating its effectiveness for privacy-preserving synthetic data generation.
