**Contributed Talk** at the **11th World Congress in Probability and Statistics (Bernoulli–IMS 2024)**, hosted by Ruhr University Bochum, Bochum, Germany.

This talk presented a semi-Bayesian nonparametric estimator of the Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD) measure. The proposed methodology combines Bayesian nonparametric learning with kernel-based distribution comparison, enabling robust goodness-of-fit testing and improving distribution matching in generative adversarial networks. The presentation covered the theoretical properties of the estimator together with empirical studies demonstrating its effectiveness in statistical inference and deep generative modeling.
