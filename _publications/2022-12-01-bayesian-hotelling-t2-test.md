## Abstract

The multivariate one-sample problem considers an independent random sample from a multivariate normal distribution with mean $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ and unknown covariance matrix $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}$. For a given real vector $\boldsymbol{\mu}_1$, the objective is to assess the hypothesis $H_0:\boldsymbol{\mu}=\boldsymbol{\mu}_1$. This paper proposes a new Bayesian approach to this problem by comparing the change in the Kullback--Leibler divergence from the prior to the posterior through the relative belief ratio. Prior elicitation is also considered. The proposed approach is illustrated through several examples.
