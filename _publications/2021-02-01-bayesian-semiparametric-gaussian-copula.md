## Abstract

Semiparametric copulas are useful tools for modeling a multivariate distribution whose dependence structure is induced by a known copula and whose marginal distributions are estimated. In this paper, we use a Bayesian semiparametric copula approach to model the underlying multivariate distribution $F_{\mathrm{true}}$. First, a Dirichlet process prior is placed on the unknown marginal distributions of $F_{\mathrm{true}}$. A Gaussian copula model is then employed to capture the dependence structure of $F_{\mathrm{true}}$. Based on this framework, we develop a Bayesian multivariate normality test by combining the relative belief ratio with the Energy distance. Several theoretical properties of the proposed approach are established, and a variety of examples, including high-dimensional settings, are presented to illustrate its performance.
