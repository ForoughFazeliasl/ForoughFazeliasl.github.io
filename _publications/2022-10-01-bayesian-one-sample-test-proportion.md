## Abstract

This paper presents a new Bayesian approach to the one-sample test for proportion. Specifically, let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ be an independent random sample of size $n$ from a Bernoulli distribution with an unknown parameter $\theta$. For a fixed value $\theta_0$, the objective is to test the null hypothesis $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$ against all possible alternatives. The proposed method is based on the well-known Kullback--Leibler divergence between two binomial distributions with a suitably chosen prior. The discrepancy between the prior and posterior distributions is quantified through the relative belief ratio, which serves as a measure of evidence. The paper also establishes theoretical properties of the proposed method and illustrates its performance through simulation studies and real-data examples.
